# Training

Shared domain language for planning and recording personal strength training.

## Language

**Exercise**:
A user's named lift, such as Bench Press, with shared muscle groups and one or more variants.

**Variant**:
A reusable configuration and progression track within an exercise, such as Heavy or High Reps. Sessions using the same variant share its targets and progression.

**Session**:
A saved, ordered plan referencing exercise variants. It is reusable across separate workouts.
_Avoid_: Workout when referring to a saved plan

**Session variant**:
A placement of a reusable variant in a session, including its order. The placement has no independent training configuration.

**Workout**:
One actual training occasion, started from a session or in free mode.
_Avoid_: Session when referring to a training occasion

**Workout exercise**:
One attempt at a variant within a workout. It starts as a draft and becomes part of exercise history on explicit completion.

**Prescribed set**:
A set expected by the variant's training configuration.

**Bonus set**:
Extra work recorded for one workout without changing prescribed volume or progression eligibility.

**Target**:
The reps or weight to aim for, separate from recorded performance.

**Recorded performance**:
The reps and weight actually performed in a workout, independent of future targets.

**Free mode**:
A workout started without a saved session, with variants added as training proceeds.

**Rep threshold**
A threshold number which determines when an exercise is ready for a weight increase.
